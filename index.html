01 / ABOUT ME

Professional profile

I am a Software Tester with over three years of professional experience in testing web and desktop applications across healthcare, pharmaceutical, engineering and education projects.

My testing experience includes manual functional, regression, exploratory, integration and localization testing, as well as requirements analysis, test documentation, defect management and collaboration with international development teams in Agile and Scrum environments.

With an engineering degree in Computer Science and a master's degree in Management and Production Engineering, I combine technical knowledge, analytical thinking and an understanding of business processes. My background in technical procurement further strengthens my ability to understand complex products, customer requirements and business needs.

I enjoy identifying problems, exploring how systems work and proposing practical improvements that contribute to software quality and user experience.

I am currently expanding my technical skills through personal projects involving web development, software testing, Linux, Docker and self-hosted IT infrastructure.

Languages: Polish (Native) · English (B2) · German (B1)
